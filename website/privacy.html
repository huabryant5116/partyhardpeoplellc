Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 9, 2026 • Last Updated: October 9, 2026

1. Introduction

Party Hard People LLC ("we", "our", or "the Company") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you visit our website or submit an inquiry for kids party entertainment and equipment rentals across Southern California.

2. Protection of Children's Privacy (COPPA & CalOPPA)

Our website and services are intended exclusively for adults (parents, guardians, and adult event coordinators) planning family or school events. We do not collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

In our Party Builder questionnaire, we ask only for child counts and broad age categories (for example, "4–7 years") strictly to recommend age-appropriate party games and music. We never ask for or record children's names, dates of birth, or individual identifiers.

3. Information We Collect from Adults

When you request a quote or contact us, we collect:

  • Adult contact details: Full name, email address, phone number.
  • Event logistics: Event date, start/end times, city or venue address, venue type.
  • Service selections: Chosen entertainment options, equipment rental quantities, theme notes.
  • Attribution data: How you heard about us, optional referral notes, and standard web traffic parameters (UTM).

4. How We Use Your Information

We use your information solely to:

  • Calculate and deliver your personalized party quote.
  • Communicate regarding event availability, logistics, and scheduling.
  • Process your 25% deposit and secure your equipment reservation upon quote acceptance.
  • Dispatch event staff and rental equipment to your designated venue.

5. No Sale of Personal Information & Text Message Terms

We never sell or rent your personal information to third parties or marketing affiliates.

If you opt in to receive text messages (SMS) regarding your quote or event updates, your mobile phone number and consent records are never shared with or sold to third parties for marketing purposes. Message frequency varies based on your inquiry. Message and data rates may apply. You may reply STOP at any time to cancel SMS communications or HELP for assistance.

6. Third-Party Service Providers

We work with trusted third-party providers who adhere to strict data security standards:

  • Payment Processing: Payments and deposits are processed via PCI-compliant hosted checkout (e.g. Stripe). We do not store credit card numbers on our servers.
  • Transactional Email & SMS: Used exclusively to deliver quotes and operational confirmations.

7. Data Retention & Your Rights

You may request to inspect, update, or permanently delete your contact and inquiry records from our system at any time by emailing us at hello@partyhardpeople.com.
