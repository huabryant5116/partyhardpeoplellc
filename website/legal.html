Legal Notices & Service Disclosures

Effective Date: October 2026 · Party Hard People LLC

1. Business Identity & Service Area

Party Hard People LLC is a registered event entertainment and equipment rental company operating throughout Southern California, specifically serving communities within:

  • Los Angeles County
  • Orange County
  • San Diego County

For inquiries, support, or notices, contact us at hello@partyhardpeoplellc.com or bookings@partypeoplellc.com, or by phone at (714) 609-5261.

2. Quotes, Pricing & Booking Confirmation

All quote estimates generated through our online Party Builder are preliminary planning estimates based on the details provided. Official prices, availability, delivery windows, and equipment configurations are verified and confirmed by Party Hard People LLC before any payment is requested.

A booking is officially confirmed only upon:

  1. Client review and electronic acceptance of the formal itemized quote; and
  2. Receipt of the required 25% initial booking deposit.

Quotes issued in writing remain valid for 7 calendar days from issuance.

3. Text Messaging (SMS) Terms of Service

By providing your phone number and selecting text message as your preferred contact method or requesting an SMS quote update, you consent to receive informational and transactional SMS messages from Party Hard People LLC regarding your party inquiry, quote, schedule reminders, and logistics.

  • Message Frequency: Message frequency varies based on your inquiry and booking activity (typically 2 to 4 messages per booking).
  • Rates: Message and data rates may apply depending on your mobile carrier plan.
  • Opt-Out: You may reply STOP at any time to cancel future text messages. An opt-out confirmation message will be sent.
  • Help: Reply HELP for assistance or contact us at hello@partyhardpeoplellc.com.
  • Condition of Purchase: Consent to receive promotional or informational SMS is not a condition of purchasing any services or equipment from Party Hard People LLC.

4. Deposit, Balance & Weather-Flex Policy

Initial Deposit: A 25% non-refundable deposit reserves your event date, entertainers, and rental inventory. The remaining 75% balance is due on or before event setup.

Weather-Flex Rescheduling: In the event of severe rain, high sustained winds (>15 mph for inflatable bounce houses), or declared weather hazards, outdoor events may be rescheduled to an agreed alternative date within 12 months with no penalty, provided notification is given at least 24 hours prior to scheduled delivery.

5. Safety, Insurance & Equipment Operation

All commercial inflatables and sound gear provided by Party Hard People LLC are inspected, cleaned, and sanitized before each event. Inflatables require adult supervision at all times during operation. Clients must ensure clear access, standard 110V power outlets within 50 feet (or request an inverter generator rental), and level ground free of debris, rocks, and overhead branches.
