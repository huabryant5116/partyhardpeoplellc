Help & FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about booking, quotes, deposits, and logistics.

How does the quote and booking process work? ▼

You can browse our packages and submit an inquiry through our Party Builder. We will review entertainer availability, travel logistics, and inventory, then email you an itemized custom quote within 24 hours. Your event date is officially reserved once you review and accept the quote and submit the 25% deposit.

What is your deposit and payment schedule? ▼

We require a 25% initial deposit to confirm your reservation and lock in pricing. The remaining 75% balance is due on the day of the event prior to entertainer setup. All quotes are backed by a 7-day price guarantee.

What happens if it rains on the party date? ▼

We offer a flexible weather reschedule policy. If inclement weather (rain or high sustained winds) threatens an outdoor party, you can reschedule your date within 12 months with no penalty, provided you notify us at least 24 hours prior to scheduled delivery.

Which areas do you deliver and provide entertainment to? ▼

We proudly serve Los Angeles County, Orange County, and San Diego County. Delivery and travel fees are calculated based on your venue location and included transparently in your custom quote.

How early do you arrive for setup? ▼

Our team typically arrives 60 to 90 minutes before your scheduled start time to inflate bouncers, position tables and chairs, and sound-check all audio and microphones.
