Terms & Booking Policies

Effective Date: October 2026 · Governing Law: State of California

1. Quotes & 7-Day Price Guarantee

Submitting an inquiry through our Party Builder generates a preliminary estimate but does not obligate either party or confirm a booking. The owner reviews the submission, verifies travel and inventory availability, and issues a formal quote. All issued quotes are valid and price-locked for 7 calendar days from issuance.

2. Deposit & Booking Confirmation

A booking is officially confirmed only when the customer accepts the quote and pays the required 25% initial deposit. Upon verified deposit payment, all requested entertainment hosts, DJs, and rental equipment are atomically reserved for your event interval.

The remaining 75% balance is due prior to the start of the event.

3. Flexible Cancellation & Incurred Costs Policy

We understand that family plans can change. In the event of a customer-initiated cancellation, Party Hard People LLC will refund amounts paid less verifiable, nonrecoverable costs already incurred on behalf of your event (such as special ordered materials, custom entertainment assets, or committed subcontracted equipment). All cancellation decisions and proposed refunds are reviewed by the business owner.

4. Weather Rescheduling Policy (12-Month Guarantee)

If outdoor weather conditions (such as persistent rain, high winds exceeding 15 mph, or official severe weather warnings) make operating inflatables or outdoor sound equipment unsafe, the customer may reschedule the event to any mutually available date within 12 months of the original event date.

100% of your paid deposit transfers to the rescheduled date with zero change fees.

5. Equipment Rental Safety & Supervision

For all inflatable bounce houses and interactive rentals:

  • Adult supervision is mandatory at all times while equipment is in use.
  • Shoes, glasses, jewelry, and sharp objects must be removed prior to entering inflatables.
  • The customer is responsible for providing a clean, level setup surface (grass, turf, or clean concrete) clear of overhead wires or underground sprinkler lines.

6. Service Area & Travel Logistics

Party Hard People LLC operates throughout Los Angeles County, Orange County, and San Diego County. Travel charges, if applicable, are based on distance from our regional hubs and are itemized in your quote before acceptance.
