Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our booking process, deposits, rentals, and policies.

1. Submit an Inquiry: Use our interactive Party Builder to describe your event type, date, location, guest count, and service preferences.
2. Owner Review & Quote: Our owner reviews your date, checks equipment availability, and sends a formal quote locked for 7 days.
3. 25% Deposit: To confirm, you accept the quote and pay a secure 25% deposit online. This atomically reserves your date and equipment.

Every children's party is unique! Venues differ (parks without power require generators, multi-story venues have special access needs), travel distances vary across Southern California, and guest counts influence staffing. Our Party Builder provides transparent starting estimates while allowing our owner to tailor an accurate, fair quote before you commit.

A 25% deposit of the approved quote total is required to secure your booking. The remaining 75% balance is due prior to the start of the event. We accept all major credit cards, Apple Pay, and Google Pay through secure hosted checkout.

We offer flexible cancellations based on costs already incurred. If you need to cancel, we calculate a refund deducting only verifiable, nonrecoverable expenses (such as custom purchased materials or committed third-party rentals). Every refund request is reviewed by the owner to ensure fair treatment.

Southern California weather is usually sunny, but if rain, severe wind, or unsafe conditions affect your outdoor party, you can reschedule your event to any mutually open date within 12 months with zero penalty. Your deposit and equipment reservations transfer seamlessly.
