Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our booking process, deposits, rentals, and policies.

How does the quote and booking process work? ▼

1. Submit an Inquiry: Use our interactive Party Builder to describe your event type, date, location, guest count, and service preferences.
2. Owner Review & Quote: Our owner reviews your date, checks equipment availability, and sends a formal quote locked for 7 days.
3. 25% Deposit: To confirm, you accept the quote and pay a secure 25% deposit online. This atomically reserves your date and equipment.

Why don't you display rigid, fixed package prices publicly? ▼

Every event is unique. Venues differ between private backyards, city parks without power, and community centers. Distances vary across Los Angeles, Orange County, and San Diego. We build customized quotes so you never pay for services or travel you do not need.

What is your weather rescheduling policy? ▼

We offer a generous 12-month weather rescheduling guarantee. If rain, high sustained winds (>15 mph), or severe weather make operating inflatables or outdoor sound equipment unsafe, you may reschedule your celebration to any open date within 12 months with zero change fees and full transfer of your deposit.

How do you handle hygiene and safety for bounce houses and microphones? ▼

Cleanliness and child safety are our highest priorities. All bounce houses are vacuumed, disinfected with child-safe antibacterial cleaners, and inspected before each use. Microphones and props are sanitized between events, and all electrical connections are protected with GFCI safety breakers.
